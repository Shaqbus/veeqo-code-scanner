Features

Everything you need to manage Amazon compliance codes at scale

🔍 Auto-Detection

Identifies items needing Transparency or Serial Number codes by detecting [TP], [SN], or [SN2] in the product title.

📱 Barcode Scanning

Works with any USB/Bluetooth scanner. Scan codes directly into the modal — no copy-paste needed.

🎯 Per-Item Tracking

Each line item tracks independently. Multi-SKU orders show separate badges per item with correct qty.

✅ Progress Badges

Color-coded: Gray (0 scanned), Orange (partial), Green (complete ✓). Visible at a glance on every order.

⛔ Ship Block

Prevents buying labels until all required codes are scanned. Zero compliance errors.

📄 Flat File Export

One-click export for Seller Central. Handles split shipments, carrier codes, ship methods, and bulk orders.

Scanning Workflow

Optimized for barcode scanners — zero clicks between orders

Click badge on item→ Scan code(s)→ Enter ↵ Upload→ Auto-advance next item→ Scan code(s)→ ⋯ repeat
💡 Pro tip: Program your barcode scanner to send Enter after each scan. The flow auto-advances: Upload → Next Item → Next Order. Never touch the mouse.

Split Shipments

When an order ships in multiple packages with different tracking numbers:

  • Each line item gets its own row with the correct tracking number
  • Codes are allocated to the correct shipment based on Veeqo allocation data
  • All required fields (order-id, order-item-id, quantity, ship-date) populated per row
  • Fully compliant with Amazon's split shipment requirements
